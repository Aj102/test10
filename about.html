ABOUT THE CFSI STUDENT PORTFOLIO

An online academic hub where Senior High School Fatimanians build profiles and showcase their best work
Explore Showcase

What Is It?

The CFSI Student Portfolio is a dedicated digital space made for Senior High School students of Children of Fatima School, Inc. Here, learners can create a profile, upload their best projects, and share them with the school community so their hard work doesn't stay buried in notebooks and folders.

Who It’s For

Designed primarily for current Grades 11 and 12 students to document their academic journey, as well as teachers and advisers reviewing student output, and parents or visitors celebrating Fatimanian excellence.

Why It Exists

  • Gives students a single place to organize and present projects, photos, documents, and videos.
  • Highlights skills and growth over time for college applications, scholarships, and future careers.
  • Inspires students to take pride in their work and learn from peers.

Key Features

  • Register using your LRN, grade level, and section.
  • Customize your student profile with personal details and photo.
  • Upload photos, PDFs, videos, documents, and web links.
  • Browse, search, filter, and rate works in the Community Showcase.
  • Search for fellow learners directly from the home page.

How It Works

  1. 1
    Sign Up: Create an account using your official CFSI school details.
  2. 2
    Upload Projects: Add your outputs, descriptions, and #tags to your portfolio.
  3. 3
    Share & Inspire: Publish your works to the Community Showcase for classmates and teachers to view.

About Children of Fatima School, Inc.

Children of Fatima School, Inc. was founded and established in October 1995 by Ma. Presentacion G. Pineda and Wilfredo R. Jesalva (who became its first principal), with the support of Mr. Alejandro F. Pineda and Mrs. Victoria G. Pineda. The school officially opened its doors and began classes in June 1996.

PHILOSOPHY

A non-sectarian institution intending to strengthen the values and characters of learners to be a better catalyst of society by loving God in serving others.

VISION

CFSI envisions learners whose values and competencies enable them to realize and utilize their full potential which may contribute to the furtherance of the quality of life in their respective communities and in the wider circle of society.

MISSION

The academic community of CFSI commits themselves to protect and promote the right of every learner to quality and complete basic education where:

  • Learners are provided with a safe and motivating environment;
  • Teachers facilitate learning and constantly nurture every Fatimanian;
  • Administrators, staff, and stakeholders share responsibilities to ensure a supportive environment for effective learning to happen and develop life-long learners.

GOALS AND OBJECTIVES

CFSI will deliver continuous, quality, yet affordable education despite setbacks experienced by educational institutions and apply flexibility relative to the capacity of the learners to engage in learning for the development of one's values and character in serving God by serving others. For this school year, the institution intends to:

  • Strategize avenues to meet the capability of learners in the delivery of learning to provide educational opportunities without compromising safety;
  • Create partnerships within and among stakeholders and establish affiliations that serve as a support system for distance learning; and
  • Improve the quality of education by consistently reviewing and updating the curriculum relevant to the needs of learners by focusing on the MATATAG curriculum and current landscape of education.
